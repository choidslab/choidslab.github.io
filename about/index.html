About Me

I am Doo-Seop Choi, a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Software Security Laboratory at Hanyang University (advisor: Prof. Eul Gyu Im). My research applies machine learning and deep learning to core cybersecurity problems — detecting malicious files and analyzing network traffic for intrusion detection.

My recent work focuses on malicious document detection, particularly PDF-based malware, including how malicious samples evolve over time and how detection models can remain robust against that temporal drift. Across all of my work I take a defensive security perspective: building practical, reliable detection techniques that hold up against real-world threats.

Before and during graduate school I worked across the security industry — from CERT operations and network security monitoring to security log processing at scale and AI research engineering — experience that keeps my research grounded in operational reality.

▸ Department of Computer Science, Hanyang University, Seoul, South Korea
